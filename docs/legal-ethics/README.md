# HSI Legal & Ethics

Human Signal Index is a **public-interest observability platform**. It exists to
help the public understand the narratives shaping civilization — never to harass,
censor, dox, or wage political attacks. This directory holds the binding charter
and policies that govern everything HSI does.

## Core ethical principles

- **Evidence first.** Every claim traces to a source.
- **Traceability always.** Provenance is visible at every layer.
- **Humanity above all.** Argue against ideas, never against private people.
- **No black-box conclusions, no unattributed analysis, no unverifiable claims.**
- **Human-in-the-loop.** People, not models, make final decisions.

## What HSI is not

- Not a censorship platform.
- Not an attack or harassment platform.
- Not a doxxing platform.
- Not a political weapon.

## How this binds the platform

These policies are enforced technically (RLS, publish gate, private evidence),
procedurally (review queue, publishing checklist), and culturally (reviewer
training). Where this charter and a feature conflict, the charter wins.
