# HSI Ethics Policy (Charter)

This charter is binding on everyone who operates, reviews for, or contributes to
Human Signal Index. It governs both the platform's design and its day-to-day use.

---

## 1. We do

1. **Argue against ideas, not private people.** Critique rhetoric, claims, and
   movements — never the private identity, family, location, or livelihood of an
   individual.
2. **Cite sources.** Every public claim links to publicly available, verifiable
   source material.
3. **Preserve provenance.** Keep raw source, AI extraction, AI interpretation,
   and human review distinct and individually recorded.
4. **Use mental-health-safe framing.** Discuss harmful ideologies in a way that
   informs without amplifying harm or providing operational detail.
5. **Care for vulnerable audiences.** Add context and safe-response guidance when
   content could affect people in crisis, minors, or the grieving.
6. **Capture only public pages.** Analyze material that is genuinely public.
7. **Keep a human in the loop.** A person reviews and approves before publication.
8. **Be correctable.** Honor good-faith correction and takedown requests
   (see [`DATA-HANDLING.md`](DATA-HANDLING.md)).
9. **Stay neutral and non-defamatory.** Describe, contextualize, and document;
   do not insult, accuse, or speculate about private intent.

## 2. We do not

1. **No harassment.** HSI is never used to harass, intimidate, or pile onto a person.
2. **No doxxing.** Never publish private personal data — home address, phone,
   private contact, employer, family, or unpublished identity.
3. **No mob incitement.** Never call for or facilitate coordinated targeting,
   brigading, or retaliation against any person or group.
4. **No censorship.** HSI observes and explains; it does not seek to silence,
   deplatform, or suppress lawful speech.
5. **No private-data collection.** No bypassing logins, scraping private groups,
   purchasing personal data, or accessing non-public communications.
6. **No political weaponization.** HSI is not used to advantage or attack any
   party, candidate, or faction.
7. **No black-box claims.** Nothing is published that cannot be traced to evidence.
8. **No amplification of operational harm.** Never republish instructions,
   targeting, or other operationally dangerous content.

## 3. The four-layer separation (non-negotiable)

```
RAW SOURCE  →  AI EXTRACTION  →  AI INTERPRETATION  →  HUMAN REVIEW  →  PUBLIC REPORT
```

Each layer is recorded and labeled. Interpretation is never presented as fact;
extraction is never presented as conclusion; raw evidence stays private unless
published through the gate.

## 4. Public-education framing

When explaining a narrative to the public, HSI presents, in neutral language:

- what the rhetoric **says**;
- what it **assumes**;
- what it **leaves out**;
- **who may be vulnerable**;
- **how to respond safely**;
- questions for **healthy debate**;
- relevant **historical**, **AI-ethics**, and **biology/cosmos** context.

## 5. Enforcement

- The platform enforces this charter via RLS, the publish gate, and private
  evidence storage.
- Reviewers must decline or escalate anything that cannot be reconciled with this
  charter.
- Violations are logged and reviewed; repeated or willful violation ends access.

This charter overrides any feature, request, or pressure to the contrary.
