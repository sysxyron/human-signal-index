# HSI Responsible Use Policy

Human Signal Index is a tool for understanding, not for targeting. This policy
explains how operators, reviewers, contributors, and the public may responsibly
use HSI, and what uses are prohibited. It works alongside the
[Ethics Policy](ETHICS-POLICY.md) and [Data Handling](DATA-HANDLING.md).

---

## 1. Intended uses

- **Media literacy** — help people recognize rhetorical patterns and framing.
- **Public understanding** — explain narratives, movements, and their context.
- **Accountability & observability** — make the influence landscape traceable.
- **Research** — support neutral, evidence-backed analysis of public signals.

## 2. Responsible-use commitments

| Audience | Commitments |
| --- | --- |
| **Operators / admins** | Enforce the charter; least-privilege access; keep evidence private; honor takedowns; maintain monitoring. |
| **Reviewers** | Verify provenance; keep framing neutral and safe; escalate harm; never publish private data; sign off only when the gate is met. |
| **Contributors** | Submit only public material with accurate attribution; never submit private data or login-walled content. |
| **Public readers** | Use reports to understand, not to harass; report errors via the correction process. |

## 3. Prohibited uses

- Identifying, locating, contacting, or targeting any private individual.
- Coordinating harassment, brigading, or retaliation.
- Republishing operational/harmful instructions or targeting content.
- Using HSI output to advantage or attack a political party, candidate, or faction.
- Circumventing access controls or attempting to expose private evidence.
- Presenting HSI analysis as a verdict on a person rather than a description of rhetoric.

## 4. Safe-framing requirements for any output

- Describe rhetoric, not people.
- Include what-it-claims / assumes / leaves out / who-may-be-vulnerable.
- Provide safe-response guidance and debate questions where relevant.
- Avoid amplification: paraphrase rather than quote operationally dangerous text.
- Cite sources and show traceability status.

## 5. Vulnerable-audience care

When content touches self-harm, despair, or crisis themes:

- Lead with safe, non-amplifying framing.
- Avoid method/operational detail entirely.
- Include supportive context and signposting to appropriate help where suitable.
- Apply extra reviewer scrutiny (risk levels 3–5).

## 6. Accountability

Misuse by an operator, reviewer, or contributor results in access review and may
end access. All privileged actions are logged in `audit_logs`. Concerns can be
raised through the contact channel and are handled under the correction/takedown
process.
